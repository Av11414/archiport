The House of Meditation

LocationTokyo, Japan
Year2025—26
ProgramExperimental retreat

The House of Meditation is an experimental architecture project designed around a compact retreat defined by compression, vertical release, and controlled light. Movement slows the body before opening into a single meditation chamber. A circular roof aperture introduces a moving beam of sunlight, making time the primary spatial element.
